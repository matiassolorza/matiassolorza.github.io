## Research

### Natural Disasters and Slow Recoveries: New Evidence from Chile
*with Lissette Briones*

This paper studies the long-run economic effects of natural disasters using new evidence from Chile, with a focus on the persistence of economic impacts and recovery dynamics.

### The Ambiguity Costs of Climate Change
*with Frances C. Moore and Benjamin Collier*

Work in progress.

This project studies how ambiguity regarding climate damages affects economic decisions and climate policy evaluation.
